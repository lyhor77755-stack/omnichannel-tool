Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2, 2026

Welcome to OmniChannel Creator. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information, access tokens, and credentials when you use our application to publish content to your Facebook Pages.

Important: OmniChannel Creator is a client-side application. We do NOT collect, store, or transmit your credentials, passwords, or Facebook access tokens to any external servers. All authentication and authorization tokens remain strictly local on your device.

1. Facebook Platform Permissions & Usage

OmniChannel Creator integrates with Facebook Graph APIs to automate and manage content publishing. To provide these features, the application requests the following specific permissions during the Facebook Login process:

  • pages_manage_posts: Allows the application to automate video publishing, create posts, and manage content on the Facebook Pages you manage.
  • pages_read_engagement: Allows the application to read page engagement metrics, check posting status, and verify that your content has been successfully published.

These permissions are used solely to allow you to upload and schedule videos directly to your Facebook Pages through our interface.

2. Data Collection and Storage

We believe in absolute data minimization. Because of this, OmniChannel Creator operates under the following privacy principles:

  • No External Storage: Any access tokens, app IDs, or user settings generated or entered within the app are stored exclusively in your local browser storage (such as LocalStorage or IndexedDB) or in memory. They are never sent to our servers or any third-party databases.
  • Direct API Communication: All API requests (including video uploads and page queries) are sent directly from your client machine to Facebook's official Graph API endpoints. There is no middleman server intercepting this data.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: We do not use tracking cookies, analytics tools, or telemetry to monitor your activities or collect usage behavior.

3. Data Protection

Since all data (including access tokens) is stored locally on your device, the security of your account credentials depends on the security of your local environment. We recommend that you:

  • Keep your web browser and operating system up to date with the latest security patches.
  • Do not share your local environment or device with untrusted parties.
  • Disconnect the application/revoke permissions via your Facebook App Settings if you choose to stop using the application.

4. Data Deletion and Revocation

You have full control over your data. To remove OmniChannel Creator's access and delete any association with your Facebook account, you can do so directly through Facebook:

  1. Go to your Facebook Profile's Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  2. Click on Apps and Websites in the left menu.
  3. Locate OmniChannel Creator and click Remove.
  4. To clear local data in the app, you can simply click "Logout" or clear your browser's site data/cache.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page with the updated "Last Updated" date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us or consult the project repository documentation.
